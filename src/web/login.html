Keep Dubarr on your home network. It can change your media files and holds your Radarr, Sonarr and Jellyfin API keys, so we don't recommend putting it behind a reverse proxy (Nginx Proxy Manager, SWAG, Traefik, Cloudflare Tunnel and the like) for access from outside your network. Use a VPN such as WireGuard or Tailscale to reach it remotely instead.

Forgotten your password? Delete auth.json from Dubarr's config folder and restart it.
